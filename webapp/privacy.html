Privacy Policy

McQCryptoNews ("the app") is a crypto market news and analytics app available as a Telegram Mini App and as a web app (mcqcryptonews.pages.dev).

What we collect

  • Account data when you sign in with Telegram or Google: your name, username, profile photo URL, Telegram ID or email address, as provided by Telegram or Google.
  • App settings: interface language and mode (Beginner/Advanced), last visit time.
  • AI assistant usage: the number of questions per day (for limits). Questions and a snapshot of the chart are sent to an AI model provider to generate the answer and are not stored by us.
  • Push notifications (only if you enable them): a push subscription identifier via OneSignal.

Trading journal

If you connect an exchange to the trading journal, your read-only API key (or Hyperliquid wallet address) is stored only on your device, encrypted in the browser storage. Requests to the exchange are signed on your device. For BingX and Bitunix, which block browser requests, the signed request (public API key and a one-time signature, never the secret) is forwarded through our server without being stored. Your trades, tags and notes stay on your device; only when you ask for an AI review is an anonymous summary of your statistics sent to the AI model provider.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, keep your settings, enforce fair-use limits, provide a subscription if you buy one, and send notifications you enabled. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Processors

Supabase (authentication and database), Google and Telegram (sign-in), Telegram Stars and Crypto Pay (payments — we receive only the payment status, amount and a transaction ID), OpenRouter (AI answers), OneSignal (push notifications), GitHub Pages (hosting). Market data is loaded directly by your device from public exchange APIs and TradingView widgets.

Your rights

You can sign out at any time and request deletion of your account and data by writing to the McQCryptoNews bot in Telegram. We delete it within 30 days.

  • Data comes from third-party sources and may be delayed or inaccurate. The app is provided "as is" without warranties.
